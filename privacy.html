Privacy Policy

RobotLight · Last updated 29 October 2026

Summary

RobotLight is made by one person, Henrik Johansson. It listens to the sound your Mac is playing, analyses it as it plays, and draws a light show from it. The sound is never recorded or saved. The app has no network code and is not allowed to go online, so nothing it hears or stores can leave your Mac.

What the app does not do

  • It does not collect personal information of any kind.
  • It does not record, save or upload audio. Sound is analysed in memory a fraction of a second at a time and then discarded.
  • It does not track you, and it contains no analytics, advertising or third-party SDKs.
  • It has no user accounts and no sign-in.
  • It makes no network connections. The app's sandbox does not include network access.
  • It does not use the microphone.
  • It does not sell or pass on information, to anyone, for any reason.

System audio

To follow the music, RobotLight needs to hear the sound other apps are playing. The first time you start listening, macOS asks whether RobotLight may record system audio. RobotLight uses that permission only to measure the sound as it plays — loudness, beats, pitch and what kind of instruments it sounds like — and to move the robot and the lights with it. Recognising instruments happens on your Mac with Apple's built-in sound analysis.

You can turn the permission off at any time under System Settings → Privacy & Security → Screen & System Audio Recording. RobotLight then hears only silence and tells you so.

What stays on your Mac

The app saves your settings inside its own sandboxed container: the instrument, stage, colours, brightness, time of day, the city you picked for the sky, mist, presets you saved, and the name of the app you chose to listen to. None of it leaves your Mac, and deleting the app removes it.

The list of cities for the sky is built into the app. Searching it sends nothing anywhere, and the app never asks for your location.

Children

The app collects no data from anyone, including children, and is not directed at children.

Your rights

The GDPR gives you the right to know what data about you is being processed, and to have it corrected or erased. Since I neither receive nor store any data about you, there is no record on my side to request or erase.

If you buy the app, Apple gives me the same aggregated sales and crash statistics every developer gets. It is anonymised and counted in totals — I cannot see who bought the app.
