Requirements

A Mac with Apple silicon and macOS 14.2 or later.

Common questions

The robot doesn't react to the music. What's wrong?

Most often RobotLight isn't allowed to hear system audio. If audio is playing but RobotLight hears only silence, a notice at the bottom of the window says so — click Open System Settings to go to the right place, turn RobotLight on under Privacy & Security → Screen & System Audio Recording, then come back to the app. Also check that the source in the Audio menu is Entire System or the app that is actually playing, and that listening is started (the menu says Stop Listening).

Which apps does it work with?

Any app that plays sound on your Mac: Music, Spotify, a web browser, a video player. Choose Entire System to follow everything, or pick a single app in the Audio menu to follow only that one. Apps that are playing right now are marked ▸. If the app you picked isn't running, RobotLight waits for it and starts listening when it opens.

Does it record my music?

No. The sound is analysed as it plays and thrown away. Nothing is saved, and the app cannot go online. See the privacy policy.

The music is playing but nothing happens on stage.

RobotLight follows the sound the app actually sends out. If the app is paused or muted inside itself, RobotLight hears silence too, and the stage rests until the music comes back.

Can I change the scene?

Under Scene in the menu bar: synth or electric guitar, a dock or a floating stage, colours, how bright the spotlights are, night, evening, day or the real time of day, mist, and the city whose sky you see. + and − walk closer to the stage or back. Save a look you like as a preset.

Keyboard shortcuts

  • Return start or stop listening
  • + / − walk closer or back
  • G / S guitar or synth
  • P next colours
  • F full screen
  • Esc leave full screen

Does it use a lot of energy?

It draws a full 3D scene, so it uses the graphics chip like a game would. Save energy (30 fps) in RobotLight → Settings halves the frame rate and cuts the app's energy use by about a third. RobotLight also drops to 30 frames per second in Low Power Mode and when the Mac runs hot, and pauses completely when its window is hidden or covered.
